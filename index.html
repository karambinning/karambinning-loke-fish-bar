A proper chippy on Loke Road.

We’re a small takeaway and seat-in fish bar on the corner of Loke Road in King’s Lynn, doing one thing — fish and chips — the way our regulars expect them.

Open three days a week: Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 11 in the morning until half-past seven at night. We’re closed Sunday to Wednesday so we can keep the fryers fresh and the fish properly looked after.

Pop in for a sit-down portion, ring ahead for a takeaway, or order over on Just Eat or Deliveroo if you’d rather not move from the sofa.

Food hygiene rating — very good (FSA, inspected Feb 2025).
